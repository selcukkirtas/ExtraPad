Questions

Before you ask in email

Which iPads does this work on?

Anything from the iPad 2 (2011) onward. iPads on iOS 15 or later get the native app with hardware video decoding; anything older connects through Safari instead — same display, a slightly simpler picture.

Does my Mac's screen content go anywhere outside my network?

No. The stream goes directly from your Mac to the iPad over Wi-Fi. There's no server in between, and ExtraPad has no cloud component at all — only the license check talks to the internet, once, at activation.

What does ExtraPad need permission for?

Screen Recording, to capture the virtual display, and Accessibility, so touches on the iPad can move your Mac's cursor. Both are standard macOS prompts you'll see the first time you launch it; input control can be turned off entirely if you only want to view.

Can I move my license to a different Mac myself?

Yes — activating ExtraPad on a new Mac deactivates the previous one in the same step. You don't need to find or reach the old machine first.

What happens when the trial ends?

Every connected iPad is disconnected until you enter a license — nothing is deleted or left half-working. Buy a license whenever you're ready and every display comes right back.
