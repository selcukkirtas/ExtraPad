Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

ExtraPad is built to need almost nothing from you. This page says exactly what the few exceptions are.

What never leaves your Mac

The thing ExtraPad actually does — capturing your screen and sending it to your iPad — happens entirely over your own local network. There is no server in between, no cloud relay, and no copy of your screen, your files, or anything on your Mac is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

What does reach the internet

  • License activation. When you start a trial or enter a license key, ExtraPad sends the key and your Mac's name to LemonSqueezy (our payment and licensing provider) to confirm it's valid. That's the only outbound request ExtraPad makes that isn't to a device on your own network.
  • Your purchase. If you buy a license, LemonSqueezy processes the payment and collects what it needs to do that (email, payment details) as the merchant of record. We don't see or store your card details — see LemonSqueezy's privacy policy for how they handle that.
  • Feedback you send us. If you use the feedback form, we receive whatever you typed and the email you optionally gave us, to answer you.

What's stored on your Mac

Your license key is stored in the macOS Keychain, tied to this Mac. Pairing codes and device approvals for your iPads are stored locally too. None of this is synced anywhere - reinstalling ExtraPad on the same Mac picks it back up; a different Mac starts fresh.

No analytics, no tracking

ExtraPad doesn't phone home with usage data, doesn't include an analytics SDK, and doesn't track what you do with it. The only requests it makes off your network are the license checks described above.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, send us a message - we'll answer directly.

This policy describes ExtraPad itself. Purchases are processed by LemonSqueezy.com, acting as our merchant of record - their own privacy policy covers payment data.
